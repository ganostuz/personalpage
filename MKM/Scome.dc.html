// OUR MISSION

The best doctors are taught by people who remember what it's like to learn.

We sit between students and faculty — collecting feedback, running tutoring, and teaching students how to teach.

[01]

Curriculum feedback

Structured surveys and dialogue with faculty on how courses improve.

[02]

Near-peer tutoring

Senior students tutor juniors through the hardest pre-clinical courses.

[03]

Teaching-skills training

Workshops on how to explain, question and give feedback well.
