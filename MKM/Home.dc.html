Who we are

Medicine is learned as much outside the lecture hall as within it.

MKM brings together six student-run committees, each with its own focus — from human rights to public health. Together we run workshops, exchanges and campaigns across the region and beyond.

Every committee has its own page, its own visual world, and its own form to get in touch or get involved.
