Our mission

We believe medicine without dignity is incomplete. Our members learn to defend both the science and the person — in classrooms, in clinics, and in the systems that decide who gets care.

01

Humanitarian medicine

Simulations and case studies on care in crisis and conflict settings.

02

Refugee & migrant health

Clinics and workshops with NGOs on access to care for displaced people.

03

Ethics & advocacy

Discussion circles on consent, equity, and the politics of health.

What we run

Three programmes, year-round
PROGRAMME A

Peace & Health seminars

Monthly talks with physicians who have worked in conflict zones and humanitarian missions.

PROGRAMME B

Community clinics

Student-run support days for asylum seekers, held alongside local partner organisations.

PROGRAMME C

Rights training

Certified sessions on documenting and responding to human-rights violations in care.
